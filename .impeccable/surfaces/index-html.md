# Surface brief: personal portfolio (home + case-study routes)

Scope: the whole static site. Visitor mode: **Experience** — the work leads from the first viewport.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints
- Hiring engineers, researchers and founders deciding in minutes whether this person builds real AI systems; then
  they verify. Action: open a case study, try the gate demo, get in touch.
- Proof: the real projects and honest limits in PRODUCT.md. Nothing fabricated; private repos are labelled private.
- Constraints: static React/Vite on GitHub Pages (works under a subpath), WCAG 2.2 AA, `prefers-reduced-motion`,
  playground is a labelled local simulation. Code-led build (no image generation here).

## Chosen direction
Construction-site safety signage, in the grammar of ISO 7010 sign shapes. Roll: seed 8a09b3d9, assigned index 3 of
seven grounded candidates. Memorable moment: a "proposal" travels through six gate signs and each flips to its verdict.
Challenger verdicts: airport wayfinding — competitive (kept: arrows locked to panel edges, sticky sign band showing
the next decision); VU-meter bridge — declined (kept: red means exactly one thing); cracktro queue — declined
(kept: no ornamental containers); indoor sun — declined (kept: one monumental element at scale); drawcord cape —
declined (kept: one stepwise control that works by tap on phones); rubber-hose cel — declined (kept: one shared motion
rhythm for every state change).
Raises: red only ever means blocked; every arrow sits on the edge it points past; no panel exists without a meaning.

## Unresolved
- Owner details and per-project contribution wording (see PRODUCT.md). Night theme is secondary to the daylight sheet.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A portfolio that is a site-entrance sign system. Each sign shape carries one verdict, so evidence, limits and
actions are readable at a glance. It refuses the dark-glow AI page, the white card grid, and the blueprint cliché.
OWN-WORLD: Cool reflective-sheeting white ground, ink black, and four meaning-locked colours: red = blocked or defect,
yellow = caution or limit or work in progress, blue = approval or action, green = permitted or verified. Rounded sign
panels with an inset border, flat pictograms on a heavy stroke, condensed signage capitals for headings, a humanist
highway sans for reading, a matching mono for paths and logs. Recognisable with all content removed.
STORY: The visitor understands in seconds that this person engineers gated, auditable AI systems and states limits
plainly; believes it because the demo runs and cautions sit beside results; then opens a case study or writes.
FIRST VIEWPORT: An overhead gantry. One monumental headline panel spans the width ("Intelligent systems, built to be
checked." with name and role on a black strap plate); beneath it hang six gate signs (resolve, permission, risk,
approval, budget, execute). Scenario chips below send a proposal through them and the signs flip to verdicts. The blue
"Explore my work" action sits at the left of the beam, "Get in touch" beside it. A sticky sign band carries nav.
FORM: Site safety signage / ISO 7010 sign grammar; position 3 of 7; seed key 8a09b3d9.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
